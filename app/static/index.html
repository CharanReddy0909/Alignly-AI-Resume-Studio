SETUP & PRIVACY

Connect your AI

You choose where your resume is processed.

✦

Google Gemini Free tier available

Get an API key from Google AI Studio ↗. Open the project's .env file and set GEMINI_API_KEY=your_key. Restart the app. Free usage is rate-limited and subject to Google's terms. On the free tier, data may be used to improve Google products.

◉

Ollama Runs locally

Install Ollama ↗. In a terminal run ollama pull llama3.2:3b. Choose Ollama in the provider picker. Ollama works without cloud AI usage fees; your computer must stay on while you use it.

⌁

Privacy & accuracy

The app does not maintain a server-side resume database. Gemini requests send your resume and job description to Google's API; Ollama requests go to the configured local Ollama instance. Files and downloads are processed in memory. Saved versions are kept in your browser's localStorage only. AI output must be reviewed for accuracy.
